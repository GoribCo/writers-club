On Mondays the Mensa smells of potatoes
and of everyone's weekend
still drying on their coats.

I take the tray with the crack in the corner —
it is mine now,
nobody else wants it.

Ahmet saves a seat with his backpack.
Sofia saves a seat with her laugh.
I bring the bread nobody ordered.

We complain about the sauce
in five languages
and agree in all of them.

At home my grandmother cooks for twelve.
Here we are twelve,
and somebody else cooks,
and it is almost the same thing.
