## Before anything else

1. **Register your address** at the local citizens' office. You will need it for almost everything else.
2. **Open a bank account** — many services expect a German IBAN.
3. **Get your student ID** and check what your semester ticket covers.

## Small things that help

| Tip | Why |
|-----|-----|
| Carry some cash | Not every small shop or bakery takes cards |
| Learn the recycling system | Your neighbours will notice |
| Join one club in week one | Friends make the winter shorter |
