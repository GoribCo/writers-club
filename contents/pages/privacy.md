## Settings

Your theme, font size and site language are saved only in your browser (local storage).

## Analytics

If enabled, this site uses Google Analytics to count visits. No account or personal data is required to read.

## Maps and videos

The map on the [Contact](/contact/) page is loaded from Google Maps only after you click **Show map**. Gallery videos from YouTube use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) and load when you open the gallery.
