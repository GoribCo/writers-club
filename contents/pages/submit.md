## Who can submit?

Any student of BTU Cottbus-Senftenberg who has come to at least one club meeting.

## What we publish

- **Stories, poems, travel writing, guides and drama**
- In **any language** — your native language is welcome, not just English or German
- Your own original work, ideally under 2,000 words

Translations of your own work (or of another member's, with permission) are very welcome too.

## How to submit

1. Bring your piece to a [weekly meeting](/events/) and read it, or send it to the club organiser.
2. Include: title, your name, your country, a one-line description, and the language.
3. We read it together, suggest small edits if you want them, and publish it here — usually within two weeks.

## Your rights

You keep the copyright of everything you write. You can ask us to change or remove your writing at any time.
