## Reading in another language

Use the language menu at the top (on phones: **Languages** in the bottom bar). If a writing exists in more languages, switch with **Also in** on its page.

## Text size and dark mode

Open the settings button at the top right.

## Finding a writing

Use [Search](/search/) — it searches titles, authors and text.
