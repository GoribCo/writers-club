## Shop smart

- **Discount supermarkets** are your friend for basics: rice, lentils, onions, tinned tomatoes, oats.
- **Asian and Turkish shops** often have spices, chickpeas and fresh herbs much cheaper than big supermarkets.
- **Buy seasonal vegetables.** In autumn and winter, cabbage, carrots, pumpkin and potatoes cost almost nothing.

## Cook once, eat three times

Make a big pot on Sunday — dal, chili, soup, curry — and portion it into boxes. Monday lunch, Wednesday dinner, Friday when you're too tired to think.

## A basic dal (serves 4, about 3 €)

1. Rinse 250 g red lentils.
2. Fry one chopped onion, two garlic cloves and a spoon of cumin seeds in oil.
3. Add the lentils, a tin of tomatoes, a teaspoon of turmeric and 800 ml water.
4. Simmer 20 minutes, stirring now and then. Salt to taste.
5. Eat with rice or bread. Share with whoever smells it in the corridor.

## Share the kitchen

Shared kitchens are the best place to make friends. Offer a taste. Someone will offer theirs back — that's how I learned to make pierogi and Brazilian rice.

> Prices change — check current offers in the supermarket apps before you shop.
