## Getting there

We took the regional train on a Saturday morning, four of us from the writers club with one shared thermos of coffee. Nobody had checked the weather. That was the first mistake.

## On the water

The canals were narrow and very green. Our guide pushed the boat with a long pole and told stories in a German dialect I understood maybe one word in five. Priya translated the jokes, badly, on purpose.

> In São Paulo I spend two hours a day in traffic. Here I spent two hours on a boat and it felt like ten minutes.

## What I brought back

- A jar of pickles I did not need
- Wet shoes
- The first line of a new story
