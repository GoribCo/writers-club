## Morning

The regional train leaves early and smells of coffee and wet jackets. Ninety minutes later the city appears all at once: domes, towers, a river wide enough to make you stop talking.

## The Frauenkirche

Inside, it is all pale colours, like the inside of a shell. A guide explained that it was destroyed in the war and rebuilt stone by stone, the old dark stones placed back among the new light ones. I looked for the dark stones for a long time. It felt like reading a sentence written by two people, sixty years apart.

## Afternoon by the Elbe

We bought pretzels and sat on the riverbank with half of Dresden. Someone's dog stole half of Sofia's pretzel. Nobody was surprised.

## Getting back

Check the last train before you start relaxing. We almost didn't.
