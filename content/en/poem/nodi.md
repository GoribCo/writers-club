---
title: "River"
author: tanvir-ahmed
country: "Bangladesh"
translator: "Tanvir Ahmed"
date: 2026-10-22
description: "Two rivers — the Padma and the Spree — and a student standing between them."
---

The Padma knew my name,
the Spree is still learning it.

The Padma's water was brown, warm,
like my mother's hands.
The Spree's water is clear, cold,
like the page of a new book.

I stand between two rivers,
one foot here,
one foot in memory.

One day perhaps
both rivers will know me.
