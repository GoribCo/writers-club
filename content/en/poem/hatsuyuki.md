title: "First Snow"
author: haruto-sato
country: "Japan"
translator: "Haruto Sato"
date: 2026-11-16
description: "Five haiku about the first snow in Cottbus."
---

first snow —
even in a town I don't know
the same white

on the bicycle
saddle it settles:
a small winter

outside the window
a night quieter
than my mother's voice

in every lit window
of the dorm
a different country

stepping on snow —
only the sound of it
still from home
