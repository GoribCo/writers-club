author: yousef-haddad
country: "Jordan"
date: 2026-10-05
description: "A two-person comedy about a broken heater and a very polite misunderstanding."
---

*A cold room. YOUSEF in a blanket, phone on speaker. A recorded voice plays hold music.*

**VOICE:** *Ihr Anruf ist uns wichtig.*

**YOUSEF:** (to the audience) It has been important to them for forty minutes.

*(A click.)*

**LANDLORD:** Hausverwaltung, guten Tag.

**YOUSEF:** Guten Tag! My heater… *Heizung*… is… (searches) …*traurig*.

**LANDLORD:** Sad?

**YOUSEF:** Very sad. It does not work. It only makes noises. Like a man complaining.

**LANDLORD:** Ah. That is normal. It is old.

**YOUSEF:** I am also old now. I have been cold since Tuesday.

*(Pause.)*

**LANDLORD:** I send someone on Thursday.

**YOUSEF:** Thursday! Thank you! (beat) Which Thursday?

**LANDLORD:** …Thursday.

*(Lights down. The heater makes a long, sad noise.)*
