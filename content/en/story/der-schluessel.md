I have lived in Cottbus for twenty-two years. I know every street, every bakery, every shortcut through the park. And one Tuesday evening in October I stood in front of my own front door without a key.

My phone was dead. My flatmate was in Leipzig. It was raining, of course.

There was a light on the ground floor. A student from Vietnam had lived there since August; until then we had only exchanged "hello" on the stairs. I knocked.

He opened the door, looked at me, wet and embarrassed, and said only: "Tea?"

We sat for two hours in his small kitchen until the locksmith came. He told me about Hanoi, about his first winter, about what it is like when nobody can pronounce your name. I told him where to buy good rice in Cottbus.

I know every street of this city. But that evening I was the guest. And it was a good evening.
